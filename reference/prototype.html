Impacts
Flooding will be possible over burn areas, low-water crossings, creeks, streams, washes and swimming holes. Larger creeks and rivers may exceed bankfull. Flooding of paved roads and highway underpasses will be possible. Unpaved roads could become muddy and impassable.

What to do

You should monitor later forecasts and be alert for possible Flood Warnings. Those living in areas prone to flooding should be prepared to take action should flooding develop.

How we use AI

Local Pulse uses a small AI model to rewrite official notices in plain language. It does not choose which notices appear or how serious they are. Our code checks every number and date against the original, and you can always read the agency's exact words. News headlines are never rewritten.

Your privacy

Your place stays on your phone. The app sends our server only a rounded version of it (to about 1 km) to find what's nearby, and we don't keep a record of it.

Why some stories are hidden

Local Pulse is about what changes your day: weather, roads, schools, health and your community. Stories about politics, elections and opinion are left out of these lists. The same rule applies to every outlet.
